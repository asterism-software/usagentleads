## Cannibalization Rules

- Do not make blog posts target the exact transactional phrase that belongs to `/` or `/states/[state]`.
- Use "buy", "CSV", "$99", "database", and state names mainly on transactional pages.
- Use "how to", "free", "lookup", "compare", "compliance", and "strategy" mainly on blog pages.
- The state directory should target "by state" queries, not national "real estate agent email list" head terms.
- State pages should not target "all states" or "full database"; they should stay focused on one state.
- The data methodology page should support trust and source-quality queries, not pricing or purchase queries.
- The campaign planner owns "agent outreach campaign planner", "agent outreach ROI calculator", and "agent outreach list size calculator" variants; it does not own recruiting or partnership economics.
- The compliance checker owns "cold email compliance checker", "CAN-SPAM checker", and pre-send compliance-review terms. The cold-outreach post continues to own educational and template queries.
- The subject line tester owns real-estate subject-line checker and preview-text variants. Email-template posts should target examples and guidance, not the tester's primary query.
- The domain authentication checker owns SPF, DKIM, and DMARC checker variants. It should not target broad inbox-placement or generic email-deliverability terms.
- The brokerage recruiting calculator owns company-dollar, recruiting-cost, and recruiting-payback queries. The partnership calculator owns professional relationship-program value queries and excludes referral-fee or RESPA legal-advice terms.
